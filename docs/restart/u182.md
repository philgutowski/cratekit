What landed. `crate job show` and `crate job list` carry `playlist` and
`source` for an intake job: the account's name for the playlist, frozen at the
queue, or for a job queued by hand the reference it was listed from; a set's
file and the hearts' reserved reference are a source and never a playlist
name. `crate job cancel` takes `--stale-days N` (one or more, a usage error
otherwise) and `--dry-run`, in place of a job id. It reads every open intake
job of the configured library (`intake_holds` in `src/cratekit/cli/jobs.py`)
with its age in whole days, its track count and the other open jobs holding
exactly its item ids, and answers three lists: cancel, kept and left. Only a
queued job queued more than N days ago whose driver lock is free is offered;
a running, blocked, younger or unreadably dated job is left with its reason.
Of several open jobs holding the same tracks the newest is kept and each copy
names it. Each cancel is the existing `jobs.cancel`, taken under the job's
driver lock while the row still says queued, and says what it frees, read as
the pass reads a playlist (`playlist_identity`, pinned by test to
`sync.playlists.recorded_as`). `--dry-run` beside a job id previews one
cancel. The `sync.land.left-open` row groups the open jobs it does not drive
by that playlist, with age, size and copies and each job's way on, and says in
one sentence that cancelling a job lets its playlist's increment queue again,
naming the dry run. A pass continued with `--pass-run` never drives a
cancelled job: it names each on an info row and lists the rest with
`intake missing --skip-failed`, and a pass whose every job was cancelled ends
`no_op` naming a new pass, never a resume. `docs/GUIDE.md` says all of this in
the Job paragraph and the stall table.

Decisions made here, the conservative call each time. "One confirmation" is
the command after its `--dry-run`, not a gate: the single cancel has none, a
cancel moves no file, and `crate job resume` reopens a cancelled job. Only a
queued job is stale; a blocked one is listed with the command that cancels it
by id. The stale form is scoped to the configured library, like the left open
row. A job whose queued time cannot be read is never stale. The newest copy of
an identical track set is kept even when it is itself stale, as the brief says,
and it still holds its playlist until it is driven or cancelled by id. "Never
a running one" is read as never a job a live driver holds: a running row no
driver holds is reconciled to queued first, as every job verb does (a
residual records the reading).
