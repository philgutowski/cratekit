**U270, #699, exporting a crate to the account adds its tracks a hundred to a
request, built in the cloud lane on 2026-11-13.** Filed from the speed survey
of `main` at `a7fcc9f4` as an idea; Phillip answered it on 2026-11-13 with
option 1: keep one job item per track, let the driver add a job's items in
batches up to the provider's limit per request, record each track only after
the provider's answer names it, and settle a partly answered batch by one read
back of the playlist. The brief named `export set`; the per item job it means

corrected on the card.

**What landed.** Three layers.
- **The engine** (`core/jobs.py`). `drive` takes an optional `Batching`
  (handler and size); `claim_batch` claims up to the size of pending items in
  ordinal order in one transaction, and the handler gets the job, the claimed
  items and a `stop` callable that reads the signal. It answers one outcome
  per item, settled by the per item branches (commit then done, declined,
  failed). A raise out of the handler is every item's outcome; a `Parked`
  among the outcomes releases the whole batch and blocks the job; a batch
  answered under a signal is released whole; an item given no outcome, or an
  outcome that is neither produced nor an exception, is failed as an internal
  error. The single item path is the same code it was, its commit now a
  shared nested function.
- **The binding** (`cli/handlers.py`). A worker that names a batch form of
  itself as `batched = (function, size)` (`BATCH_ATTRIBUTE`) is driven in
  batches with the frozen configuration bound; `batching_of` refuses a form
  that is not a callable and a size of at least one. An attribute of the
  resolved worker rather than a second table, so a substituted worker is
  driven one item at a time. Only `export.playlist` names one, at
  `outbound.export.BATCH`, 100, the adapter's `ADD_CHUNK`.
- **The writer.** `export.cli.handle_batch` and `run_export_items` plan each
  track alone (a row the export no longer carries, or a match that raised,
  fails that item alone; a park is raised) and write the batch with one
  `export.apply`, which reserves once, reads the playlist once and sends what
  it lacks in one `add_tracks`. Each row's outcome is `added`, `already` or
  `refused`, a track recorded only when the answer or the adapter's one read
  back names it, in one ledger write after the answer
  (`ledger.record_playlist_tracks`). A refused track fails its item with the
  cause `add_to_playlist` raised for it. Two rows matched to one track send it
  once and read as added then already, as two items did. The create, the
  adoption and the filing are said on the first item reported only.
  `run_export_item`, the per item worker, is one item of the same code.
