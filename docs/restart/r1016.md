**R-1016 built, 2026-10-14, on `relay/322`, issue #322:** an account
playlist listing and a folder playlist listing now cost their pages and
nothing more. The cause was in the provider client, not in the walk: its
playlist parser, `Playlist.parse_factory`, ends in `Playlist.factory`, which
rebuilds every playlist the logged in account created as a `UserPlaylist`,
and that constructor sends `GET playlists/<id>` to read an etag. Followed
playlists skip it, which is why 624 listed cost 637 and not 1,248.
`listing.account_playlists` now sends the client's own
`users/<id>/playlistsAndFavoritePlaylists` request through the user's
request object, moves `created` onto each playlist as `dateAdded` as the
client does, and parses with `Playlist.parse`; `listing.folder_playlists`
parses with `Playlist.parse` in place of `parse_factory`. Paging, the empty
page and cursor stop rules, the page caps and the dedup by identifier are
unchanged. An answer that is not a page is refused by name rather than read
as the end, and the walks that send the client's request themselves share
one rejected signature translation, `_send`, naming the walk.

**The rule as it now stands:** a listed playlist is a `Playlist`, never a
`UserPlaylist`, and carries no etag. It reads its tracks as before
(`playlist_tracks` pinned over the real client). No caller edits through a
listed object today; one that wants to must open the playlist by
identifier, as `tidal_account` already does for every write.
