**What the measurement found, none of it in the brief.**

1. *The job never reached the pool.* `handle_run` analysed one file per item
   in the driver's own process; `light.run` and `run_batch`, which plan a
   pool, had no caller outside the tests. The run was serial on every
   machine, whatever its swap.
2. *The light worker's declared cost was thirty times low.* 64 MB was
   declared. A fresh process per track held 374 MB at 45 seconds, 874 at 4
   minutes, 1.85 GB at 10 and 2.0 GB at 11. A ceiling from the cores at 64 MB
   would have planned nine 2 GB workers on the 18 GB machine.
3. *Half of every track was two median filters,* and scipy's one dimensional
   rank filter returns the same medians, bit for bit, about ten times sooner
   than the two dimensional call librosa makes.

**What landed.**

*The same separation, sooner* (`analysis/separation.py`). The harmonic part
the key reads, and the harmonic and percussive pair the energy pass reads,
with librosa's own arithmetic: the medians a contiguous line at a time, the
mask a block of columns at a time so the transform, its phase, both medians,
both masks and both products are never alive at once. Held equal to
`librosa.effects.harmonic` and `librosa.effects.hpss` with `array_equal`.
Under sixteen frames, a third of a second, librosa does not equal itself
from call to call, because scipy's filter is unreliable on a line that
short, so those lines are taken by the definition. This is the 2.2 times in
the table and it holds on a machine refused a pool.

*The measuring runs ahead; the writing does not move* (`core/pool.py`
`stream_workers`, `analysis/ahead.py`). The pool gains a third seam beside
`plan_pool` and `map_workers`: the same plan, spawn, generations and in
process rule for a plan of one, with each result read by position while the
workers run on. `analyze run` opens one over the files its drive will
measure. The driver still claims one item at a time, in order, under the
driver lock, and still makes every write itself, the tags, the rename and
its intent, the report and the row; it reads the item's head from a worker
when one stands and measures it itself when not. A head measured ahead is
used only if the file's device, inode, size and modification time are what
the worker saw before it read, and the calibration, tempo window and drift
cut are the ones the item binds. A changed file, changed settings, a worker
that raised, a pool that broke, a plan of one worker, a detector a test
bound, and a job nobody opened a session for all end in the line that ran
before this unit. Stream workers ignore the terminal's interrupt, so the
driver decides where a stop lands as it always did, and leave when their
parent is killed.

*An honest cost and a ceiling from the cores* (`analysis/light.py`,
`core/cores.py`). A light worker is declared at 320 MB plus 1.5 MB for each
second of the batch's longest track, above every peak measured after the
separation's blocks (345 MB at 45 seconds, 1175 at 11 minutes). A length the
header will not give is bounded by the file's size. The ceiling is the cores
less two, read in `core/cores.py`, the one place a core count is read, and
handed to the planner as its ceiling; the planner's reserve, headroom and
swap and used percent gates decide underneath it, and the driver's vocal
model is kept back from the light pool's budget. `core/pool.py` itself still
reads no core count.

*The check line* (`analyze.pool`). Every drive records the pool it ran with
beside its reports, and the answer carries it as a check a person reads
without asking for json: the workers, and what held the count there, the
swap gate with its reading and limit, the used percent gate, the memory
available against the cost, the ceiling, or the item count. A resume of a
finished job reads the same record. A run that failed or was stopped carries
the sentence on its refusal.

**Left as it was, with the number that says why.** The vocal pass: out of
the card's scope, one model in the driver, its cap untouched. The drift: read
over the whole track, 2 percent of a run. The two decodes: 2 percent each,
and one decode would have to cross the process boundary. Energy: already
measured over the central two minutes; its time was its own separation, now
the shared one. Key from windows and key without the separation: built as
`detect.KEY_WINDOWS` and `detect.KEY_PLAIN` and NOT the default, because
their bar, agreement with rekordbox's keys within one percentage point of
the whole track's, can only be read on the library.

**What only the Mac can say.** Everything above is a four core Linux
container on synthetic audio with an untrained vocal model. The real stage
table, the real model's time, the pool on eleven cores against the machine's
real swap, a light worker's peak memory under macOS, and above all whether
the windowed key or the key without the separation agrees with rekordbox's
keys: none of these is measured. On the machine as the card found it, 11.8
GB of swap, the pool is refused by the swap gate and the run is the one
process figure, about 2.2 times faster, with a check line that says why.

**Decided, recorded here.** The run ahead lives in the handler's module and
the pool's seam rather than in the job engine, so `core/jobs.py` is
untouched and every other queued verb is as it was. The core count is read
in a module of its own so that `core/pool.py`'s rule, no core count, still
holds as written, and two guards hold the new one: the four spellings appear
in no other shipped module, and the reader's answer is used only as
`plan_pool`'s `ceiling` argument. The pool's record is a file beside the
reports because a worker's facts reach an envelope only through one; a
drive that measured nothing does not replace the record of one that did.
The cheaper key methods are a parameter with the shipped method as its
default rather than a configuration key, so nothing a person can set changes
a result before the measurement exists.
