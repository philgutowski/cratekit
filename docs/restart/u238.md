**U238, #646, a label sync question counts only the label's own releases
past its window, built in the cloud lane on 2026-11-10.** Filed from an
attended label pass on Cratekit 0.15.1 that added Atipic, Amphia, Cronos
Barac, Rawax and Gettraum. A medium card: it adds no path that writes or
deletes, and changes the count one existing write, the offer round, records.

**The cause.** `sweep.list_for_label` set `beyond=len(listed) - examined`,
every release the crawl listed past the window. The crawl lists every
release of every artist on the label's roster, verified or not, so Atipic
said 414 more for about 27 of its own, Gettraum 162 for about 9. The failing
test came first (`98216c4`): a roster of two artists, one crediting the
label and one another label, with two releases carrying no copyright line,
said 17 past a window of three where seven are the label's and two
unchecked.

**What landed.**

- **Counted by the line.** `sweep._past_window` reads each release past the
  window by its own copyright line, with no provider call, through
  `harvest.labels_from_copyright` and `verify.labels_match` against the
  sweep's needle (`entry.match or entry.name`), the two pieces the
  provider's verifier and `_credits_label` are made of. A line naming the
  label counts; a line naming another label is left out; no line counts as
  unchecked.
- **The fields.** `LabelListing.beyond` is the most the label could still
  offer, its own past the window plus the unchecked, so a release whose
  line the listing omitted is never dropped; `LabelListing.beyond_unchecked`
  is the part with no line. `review.record_round` records `beyond`, now
  without another label's releases.
- **What a person reads.** `sync_cli._past_window` is the one phrase for
  the question (`--next`, the walk's prompt and its listing) and the
  nothing new line: "7 more of the label's release(s) past this run's
  window, and up to 2 more not yet checked", or alone "up to N more past
  this run's window, not yet checked". Nothing past the window says nothing,
  never "0 more", and an unread listing still says so beside it.
- **A saved question.** `Question` saves `beyond_unchecked`. One saved by an
  earlier build has no such key, and one with an unreadable value, and
  either reads its whole figure as unchecked, so an old 414 is said as a
  bound and never as the label's.

**Decided by the session, recorded here.** `_credits_label` is not imported
because it lives in the provider adapter, which the generic sweep must not
import, and it folds "no line" into no; the sweep uses the same two pieces
directly (a residual records the three copies). `beyond` keeps its name and
its place in the review store as the upper bound, rather than becoming the
label's own count, so the grouped review list never drops a release whose
line is missing.
