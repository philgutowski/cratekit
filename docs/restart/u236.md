**U236, #628, a favourite put in on the account and taken off again before the
next organize is unfavourited, built in the cloud lane on 2026-11-07.** Filed
from sitting 88 (A90, #518) on v0.13.1. A large card: it changes what organize
writes to the library and the outbound ledger and what the Favorites edit rule
decides, and it adds a field to the frozen mirror plan.

**The cause, read off the code and driven.** A FAVOURITE put in `All
Favorites` alone settles each Favorites playlist where both sides will agree
after the write (`Decision.settle`, from the plan's reading). Its month is the
library's choice, made at the write, so at the plan neither side holds it
there and the month is settled WITHOUT the track. The account area was planned
before the write (R-1029), so the account's month never gets it that run. The
next refresh reads rekordbox holding the track in a month the snapshot lacks,
the shape of a person's addition, and an UNFAVOURITE in that window is kept by
"an addition beats a removal". The same happens for a month the write made,
which no mirror record holds yet. The card's comment that the account's month
kept the track one more organize after the carried unfavourite is the same
one run lag, R-1029, and is not this card's.

**What landed.**

- **A placement.** `organize/mirror_edits.write` reads the time of the
  membership each FAVOURITE's month placement made
  (`account_mirror.membership_times`, the reader's own `_times`), and
  `settle` records, for a FAVOURITE put in All Favorites alone, that month as
  the library's own placement: `ledger.settle_mirror_tracks(..., placed=)`,
  under the same lock, on the mirrored folder's record as `placed`, account
  title to track to time, mirrored yet or not. A track a settle gives its word
  on a title loses its placement there first.
- **Read by its membership.** `account_mirror._read_edits` hands
  `Mirrored.placed` only the placed tracks whose newest live membership on that
  playlist carries the recorded time, so a month deleted and made again, or a
  membership a person took off and put back, is the person's.
- **The rule.** `_decide_favorites` never counts a placed month as an addition
  in rekordbox: not against an UNFAVOURITE, and not as a move against a
  FAVOURITE's later change rule. A favourite put in another month on the
  account leaves the placed month (`away`), the person's choice replacing the
  library's, and the month the account asked for is never read as rekordbox's
  move against it.
- **Dropped when the account catches up.** `record_mirror_agreed` keeps a
  placement through `mirror_edits.placed_after` until the account holds the
  track there with no hold on it; a refused add keeps it. The ledger's
  normalizer keeps `placed` across every write and drops one it cannot read.
- **The two acts (U222).** `mirror_frozen` freezes each folder's placements
  beside the snapshot and overlays them, `VERSION` 7, so a version 6 record is
  planned afresh.
