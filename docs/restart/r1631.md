**R-1631, #770, the title matcher reads a title by its own words, never a
word cut out of it, built in the cloud lane on 2026-11-12.** Filed at the
review of #713 (R-1534). Large: the matcher decides which catalogue row's
genre is written onto a file, for good.

**What failed, reproduced on the base.** The shared `_title_matches` in
`tagging/searchers.py` stripped a trailing bracket from the first "(" to the
last ")" and compared by raw substring, and Beatport's `_beatport_best` did
the same on its own. A mid title bracket cut "Knee (Not Just) Deep (Part 1)"
to "knee", in "Knee Slapper"; a containment between whole titles read a word
in one title's brackets as the other ("Intro" in "Sunrise Mood (Intro)"),
refused only for a hand list of version words that missed "vocal",
"acapella", "reprise"; and characters put "Club" in "Nightclub" and "Original
Mix" in "Sunrise Mood - Original Mix". The tests written first,
`tests/test_title_matcher.py`, were 113 red on the base.

**What landed.** A title is read by its readings (`_readings`): tuples of
whole words once it has shed the bracket groups it ends with, read balanced
from the end, round or square; a dash part, on a catalogue row only a tail
that reads as a version, on the file's side also any later part, since the
live library writes `KEY - BPM - ` into the title tag and an untagged file is
asked by its name, a head being numbers and keys with a Camelot key among
them; a feature credit; a trailing run of `core.tombstone.VERSION_WORDS`,
with a year only beside a remaster; a zero padded track number; its leading
groups, which never meet another title's leading groups shed. Two titles
match on a shared reading. `_contains` and the hand list `_VERSION_WORDS`
are gone, and the list now only sheds, except a file's dash tail of version
words alone, recorded below. Beatport ranks the title without its head: the
name with its mix, then the core, then a shared reading; the lowest tier,
a row name anywhere in the whole title, is gone. Bandcamp reads a heading
whose head names the artist by its rest only. The key check reads
`core.naming.is_camelot`, the one parser `tests/test_naming.py` allows.

**Kept, and measured.** An unfiltered differential of `main`'s matcher
against the branch over the string constants in title positions in the
suite (1,151 titles, 1.3 million ordered pairs) and over every string
constant in the tagging tests (3,162, ten million pairs), and Beatport's
scorer over the first set with three mixes. Every gain and every loss whose
two sides a liberal reading calls the same recording was read by hand. The
gains are the same recording under another spelling: a headed title tag,
a square bracket suffix, a brackets alone title against its row with a
feature or a dash version. The losses are the card's shapes, other titles
that shared a word ("Dub" and "Dub Siren", "Track 2" and "Track"), rows no
catalogue writes (a `KEY - BPM` head or an "Artist - " prefix on the row's
side), and the misses recorded below. Reading the gains found four defects
of the build's own draft before review: a square bracket suffix, a track
number stem, an artist part meeting a Bandcamp heading's head, and a headed
title that is a version word.

**Decided here, recorded.** A title written only inside the other's
trailing brackets is refused, "How Does It Feel" against "Untitled (How Does
It Feel)", since its evidence is the card's wrong shape; asked of Phillip on
the card as a Question for Phillip, the build carrying on with refusing.
"with" is not a feature credit. Code conventions (the readings, their
helpers, the test tables) were the session's.
