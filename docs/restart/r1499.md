**R-1499, #665, `crate sync land` lands only its own pass's tracks, built in
the cloud lane on 2026-11-11.** Filed at the review of #655 (R-1467). Large:
it adds refusals on the path that ends at a library landing.

**What failed, reproduced on the base.** Every download stages into the one
folder and `intake land` lands the whole folder. `crate sync land` ran its
tail and planned its landing over whatever the folder held, so a label go's
download waiting at its own landing's gate (renamed by its tail), a plain
`acquire download`, an earlier pass's unlanded download, or a stray file
landed under the pass. Thirteen of the sixteen tests written first failed on
the base, with the reading lifted and `sync land` unchanged.

**What landed.** R-1467's reading of the folder moved out of `label/go.py`
whole into `src/cratekit/intake/beside.py`, which both verbs import; the go
keeps `beside`, `keep_tail` and `kept_tail` as thin wrappers and its suite is
unchanged but for the module a test patches and two detail keys. The kept tail
record is generalized from one go job to a record path and a set of own jobs,
and a sync pass's download (a download job whose list is `<pass>.list.json`,
the pass `<run>_synced`) is named with `crate sync land --pass-run RUN`, by
both verbs, as is a pass whose tail may have renamed files no download names.
`cli/sync_land.py` reads the folder before its download and again before its
tail (`_alone`), and refuses `refused-by-policy` naming each download and how
it lands; its own are the downloads over its own list (`_own_downloads`) and
the files its own tail left, kept under its pass in `sync-pass-tails` at every
tail stop (`_keep`), none once its pass has a landing proof. The tail runs in
two halves, through `name apply` and then the landing, and between them, and
again when the landing stops at its gate, the folder is compared with what it
held as the tail started (`intake.beside.settled` and `arrived`): a download
another process finished, or a file a person put there, during the tail
refuses before the landing is handed on, and no record is kept. The same
check wraps the go's tail in `cli/label_sync.py`. A folder or a library root
that cannot be read refuses.
