**U137 built, 2026-10-12, on `relay/314`, issue #314: the account filing runs
alone, `crate organize --only file`.** `--only KIND`, repeatable, narrows both
areas to the step kinds it names: `adopt`, `rotate`, `record-labels` on the
database side, `labels`, `sets`, `marks`, `file` on the account, and `yours`
on both. An area none of whose kinds is named is not planned, not asked and
not written, so `--only file` reads no backup acknowledgment and no database
and plans no label year's catalogue adds (R-913). A planned area keeps its own
preconditions; its gate shows only the named steps, and the selection is
named in its facts and in its digest document, so an answer over the whole
area is stale for a narrowed one. With no `--only` the digest is byte for
byte U112's. An unknown kind is refused by the parser, by name, before the
config is read. **Where it lives:** `organize/steps.py` (`selection`,
`Area.only`, `Area.wants`), each area's `STEPS` and its `plan_area(only=)`,
and the verb in `organize/cli.py`. **Two rules a reader would not guess:**
`yours` still has the filing and the rotation planned behind it, into an area
nobody is shown or with their step left off, so the author's listing is the
full verb's; and a rotation that refuses is shown under `--only yours`,
because its refusal is why that listing is short. **A changed rule:** the
filing now refuses, with the registry's own cause, when the label registry
cannot be read and the labels module is on. Before, it planned over no
labels, and a playlist named for a followed label but not product made could
be moved into `Mine` with the label step's refusal the only clue, which
`--only file` would not even show. **Considered and not done:** a selection of
