**What landed.**

- **The reader** (`adopt/placements.py` `placements_from`) reads, in the same
  pass over the same nodes as the competing subs, the places each track holds
  beside its band per bucket, `Placements.alongside` (and those held under
  another name only, `alongside_unnamed`), and the other playlists of that
  bucket's own folder holding it, `Placements.others`. The places beside a
  band are `plan.alongside_places`: the additive derived sub classes
  (`plan.derived_classes`, `with vox` and Favorites in the shipped template,
  by the rule `vocal_sub` already applied, which now asks it) and the
  bucket's genre subs (Rominimal). The folder is the one the bucket's own
  places answer from by identifier (`plan.in_own_folder`: a split place and
  a declarer's former place sit in another folder).
- **The move across genre folders** (`adopt/move.py` `moved_out_of`,
  `leaving_moved`, `alongside_left`) puts those places into its `remove` and
  `holds`, so one move takes the row out of the old folder's band, `with
  vox`, genre sub and Favorites; a row holding only places beside the band
  there is asked too. **The genre Favorites stays** while anything else keeps
  the row in that folder (a band of its own not being left, Candy, a playlist
  made by hand), since `genre_favorites.plan_refresh` files a favourite by
  every other playlist of its folder and the next refresh would put it back
  (`plan.mirrored_classes`, asked of `favorites_class`). A band the new
  genre's bucket settles (Soul's split place of R&B's bands) counts as left
  for that rule. A place beside the band held under another name only lists
  the row under `held-under-another-name`.
- **The worker** (`adopt/move_cli.py`): `remaining` admits the places beside
  the band as leavable for a move across buckets only; its done check and
  `read_back` share `still_to_leave`, what this move was approved to remove
  that the tree still holds, a place beside the band only while
  `alongside_left` still names it; `handle` spends a genre move only when
  every move of that track in the job finished.
- **The caveat** in `tag set`'s answer and in GUIDE, which told the person to
  remove the leftover in rekordbox, is replaced by what the move does, and
  the answer now names the follow up for a row the move files itself (the
  next `adopt apply` files its `with vox`, `favorites refresh` its
  Favorites).
- **Tests**: 17 new in `tests/test_tag_set_move.py`, the brief's scenarios
  driven through the dispatcher: a vox tagged favourite filed by `adopt
  apply` (tag rows and the calibrated cut seeded as the vocals verb leaves
