**What landed.**

- **The moves are sent blind** (`modules/outbound/tidal_account.py`).
  `order_tracks` plans every move from one judged reading (`_settled`, as
  before) and sends them with no read back between, each position worked out
  from the reading with the moves before it applied. A 412 pauses, opens the
  playlist again for its current tag and sends the same move again, at most
  `ORDER_REREADS` times a move. A new `begun(start, end)` callback runs once
  before the first move; whatever it raises sends nothing. It answers the
  order the moves leave, as sent. `settled_order(playlist_id, wait=...)`
  pauses, then takes one judged reading, None when its window lags. Both are
  on the `RemoteAccount` protocol and recorded in `read_backs` (`"moves"`
  with `moved` and `stale`, `"settle"`). U220's per move read back and its
  `no-progress` refusal are gone, since a plan made once cannot loop.
- **The write records and settles** (`sync/pair_cli.py`). `begun` marks and
  carries a `moving` step (when, the reading moved from, the order left), and
  every move accepted marks `moved`. A pair whose order was sent is not
  recorded yet: once every pair is written, `_settle` reads each order once,
  waiting only what is left of `SETTLE_SECONDS` (30) from its last move. Read
  as the moves leave it, the snapshot is recorded and the pair is done; else
  the pair is pending, its reading kept on the pair (`PairStore.see`), and the
  run ends ok with `sync-pair-order-pending` naming it. A continue whose pair
  has `moving`, in its steps or (round two's fix) in the carried record
  beside them, sends no move again and only reads. A write that keeps no
  record (its plan could not be held) sends no order and stops that pair
  alone, `sync-pair-steps-unwritten` (round one's fix).
- **The next plan reads a pending order** (`_pending`). Shown as the moves
  leave it (`pair_stopped.shows`, tracks added or taken off since left out),
  it finishes the pair through U225's reading of the stopped write, nothing
  moved. Otherwise the reading must agree with one kept on the pair, taken
  after the moves and at least `AGREE_AFTER` (five minutes) before; until
  then the pair is held whole under the new per pair cause
  `sync-pair-account-settling` (exit 41) and its reading kept. Agreed and on
  the moves' path, a write stopped part way is finished from the leading
  side as U225 does; when every move was accepted it is still a lag and
  stays held. Agreed and neither, the person's change, merged as theirs.
  `Pair.seen` clears whenever the pair's snapshot moves.
- **The scope.** The account order is written only for a pair whose rekordbox
  playlist is under the top folder `GIG READY` (not a playlist of that name),
  unless `outbound.pair_order_every_pair = true` (`core/config.py`,
  validated). Outside it the gate line says so and membership syncs as
  before; rekordbox still takes an order the account leads with.
- **The forecast** (`sync/pair_calls.py`) counts the client's hidden
  `_reparse` GET after every add chunk, removal request and move
  (`REPARSE`), the order's one judged reading, its moves and its settle
  reading, and a continue's sent order as its settle reading alone.
- `docs/GUIDE.md` (the order's behaviour and the config key) and the version
  tag learning in `docs/solutions/integration-issues/` say what an order does
  now, and the one exposure left.
