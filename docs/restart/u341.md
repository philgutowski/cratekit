in the cloud lane on 2026-11-23.** Filed from the attended sitting that

**What landed.**

- `Registry.of_genre(genre)` and `Registry.genres()` in `label/registry.py`:
  the labels of one group in registry order, matched NFC then casefold as a
  name is, and each carried genre once as its first label spells it.
- `genre_group_or_refuse` in `label/cli.py`, shared by both verbs. A genre no
  followed label carries refuses `label-unknown` (already the module's
  reason, so no reserved cause pin moved), naming the genres the labels
  carry, and adds that the library's taxonomy routes the word only to its
  fallback when it does; an unreadable taxonomy leaves that clause out
  rather than hiding the refusal. A shapeless genre refuses
  `label-name-invalid` in a group's words. With no genre carried the step
  says to set one, and with no label followed to add one.
- `label registry list --genre GENRE` lists the group; `count` counts the
  group and `registered` the whole registry. Every other field flag is still
  refused on `list`.
- `label sync --genre GENRE`, at the terminal and with `--next`: a line names
  the labels asked, the estimate and the budget refusal speak of the group
  alone, the closing lines say "of genre", the run after a `quit` or a prompt
  nobody can answer is told to name the genre again, so it resumes inside the
  group, and an answer for a label in a group that still stands points back
  into it. Every genre in a printed command is quoted with `shlex.join`.
  `--genre` beside `--go`, `--answer` or `--label` refuses as a selection:
  `--go` stays the one gate over every saved answer, as the brief says, and a
  genre walk ends at that gate too.

**Conservative calls, as the plan on the card says.** A genre a label carries
is asked even when the taxonomy routes it only to its fallback (a genre kept
with `--accept-fallback`), since refusing it would leave that group
unaskable; an undeclared genre no label carries refuses and says it is
undeclared. `--go` takes no genre.
