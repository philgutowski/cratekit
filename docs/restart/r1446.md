**What landed.**

- **A move of unread tracks alone is no reorder** (`_readable_moved` in
  `sync/pair_cli.py`). After `_moved_since_stop` reads a reorder, `plan_pair`
  asks whether any readable track moved: for a stop that sent nothing, whether
  the readable tracks are off the write's path (`pair_stopped.reached` over the
  readable reading); otherwise whether the readable tracks of the settled
  reading and of the reading now differ in order (`pair_stopped.reordered`
  over both with the unread ids left out). With no settled reading the
  reorder stands as read. When no readable track moved, the reorder and
  `moved_nothing` are cleared and the stopped write is finished, so rekordbox
  keeps its order and the account is put in it.
- **Each unread track the person moved keeps the place they gave it, and
  only those** (`_unread_moved`, the review's two rounds). The moved unread
  ids are those outside the heaviest run the settled reading, or for a stop
  that sent nothing the write's start, and the reading now share, every
  readable id weighed above all unread ones together so the readable tracks
  are held where they stand: a block dragged together is moved whole, and one
  nobody moved stays unmoved whatever lands beside it. `plan_pair` passes the
  stopped write's target to `_with_unread_placed` with only the moved ids left
  out, so each of those stays behind the track it now follows and any other
  goes where the stopped order was taking it (R-1432); when none can be told
  apart no target is passed. The plan carries `stop_unread_moved`,
  `stop_unread_others` and `stop_unread_unsent`.
- **The gate says so.** The pair's facts line says only tracks the account
  lists with no title or no artist were moved since the stop, or on a stop
  that sent nothing that the account stands off its path only by where they
  stand, so each one moved stays where it was put, any other goes where the
  stopped order was taking it (only when there is one), and the rest of its
  order is finished. R-1432's clause in the facts line and in the
  `sync-pair-unread` warning is shown only when another unread track is
  placed by the target. The FINISHING note says only tracks the account
  cannot read were moved since, or stand off its path, so it is not read as a
  reorder.
- No refusal, check or guard changes. `_moved_since_stop` and its settling
