**R-1372, #591, a write stopped part way through a pair's order is kept
past the age a held plan is kept for, until the pair's snapshot passes it,
built in the cloud lane on 2026-11-06.** A large card: it changes what the
account write of `crate sync pair` records as it begins an order and what
the next plan reads, on a path that ends in a rekordbox write. Filed by
U225's round two review: a stop whose run's steps were older than `KEEP`,
thirty days, was planned as before U225, and the next sync put the stopped
write's scramble into rekordbox with nothing said.

**What landed.**

- **A pair's steps carried past its run.** `core/held_plan.py` gains a
  carried store: `carry(steps, key)` writes one item's steps whole and
  atomically into `carried/` beneath the held plans, which neither `prune`
  (it lists the top directory only) nor `release` reaches; `carried()` reads
  every record whatever its age, each as its run's `Steps` holding that one
  item, and names a record it cannot read, of another version, out of its
  place or naming another run, and a directory it cannot list (listed with
  `iterdir`, since `glob` swallows an `OSError`); `let_go(run_id, key)`
  removes one.
- **Carried as the order begins.** The account write carries the pair's
  steps right after it marks `STEP_ORDERING` and before the first move, and
  refuses `sync-pair-steps-unwritten` when it cannot, as an unrecorded step
  does, so a write that cannot keep the record moves nothing.
- **Let go when the pair is finished or passed.** `apply_pair` lets its own
  record go once it marks the pair done, and `_record` lets go every carried
  record of the pair that began at or before a snapshot it records whole
  (`pair_stopped.passed_by`). A record that cannot be let go is left and read
  as no write, since the snapshot is later than it.
- **Read with the kept steps.** `_Reader.stopped` reads the kept steps and
  the carried records together, a carried copy giving way to its own run's
  steps while those are kept under the same plan, since they say whether the
  pair finished; an unread carried record joins `sync-pair-stopped-unread`.
  `_Reader.stopped_write` also refuses a write that began before the pair
  was marked, since a carried record outlives a pair forgotten and marked
  again.
- `docs/GUIDE.md` says the stop is remembered however long the wait, until
  a sync finishes the pair.

**Decided by the session.** Questions of structure, recorded here: the
brief's first approach, a record of its own, rather than a gate note over an
aged record, since it keeps the order right rather than leaving it alone;
the record is written in the write path as the order begins, not carried at
prune time, because the brief's producer is thirty days with no sync, so
nothing would run to carry it; the store is generic in `core` (one item's
steps), and the pair rules stay in `sync`. A stop recorded before this
landed has no carried copy and is read from its steps for thirty days as
before; it is not migrated.
