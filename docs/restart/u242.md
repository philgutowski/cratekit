**The ledger.** `sync_marks.json` (`sync/mark_record.py`) is reused rather
than a second store, as the brief asked: version 2 adds a `taken` list, one
entry per playlist id with the title less any prefix, who took it (a pass,
`label mark`, `export archive`, or the migration), how (`pass`, `label`,
`set`, `found-prefixed`), when, and a released stamp. A version 1 record reads
as one with nothing taken and is rewritten as version 2 on its first write; an
unreadable taken list, an unknown kind or a duplicate id refuses rather than
reading as empty. `playlists.is_taken(id, name, taken)` is the one predicate
every reader asks: the record holds the id, or the title still carries the
legacy prefix, so no state exists in which a playlist taken today reads as new.
`exclusion` answers `already-swept` from it, so a stripped playlist is excluded
exactly as it was while prefixed.

prefixed playlist (`found-prefixed`, by `migration`) and every standing prefix

nothing. **The cursor is counted and not copied**, the builder's call: the
cursor already is the product's own record and excludes every one of its
playlists as `recorded`, and copying one whose pass still owes rows would take
it out of `sync mark`'s plan as though it were finished.
