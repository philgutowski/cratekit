**The split.** Every name a caller used stays importable from the module it
always came from.
- `core/config.py` (5,521 to 1,154) keeps the failure types, since the
  reserved causes guard excludes `cratekit.core.config.ConfigError` by
  module; `from_dict`; migration, loading, saving and update; and the durable
  write and lock helpers, which the suite patches on `config` and which call
  each other. It re-exports the rest.
- `core/config_base.py`: the schema version, the volume and template keys,
  the environment variables, `config_dir`, `installation_state_dir`,
  `is_inside`, `_dig`, `template_name_is_usable`.
- `core/state_files.py`: the inventory, its vocabulary and resolvers,
  `config_path`, `provider_gate_dir` and the library directories. Each entry
  is `_installation(key, relative, why)`, never restored by the rule its
  section states, or `_library(key, relative, snapshot, why)`; every field
  but `why` is equal to `c919209`'s entry for entry, and every `why` is cut to
  its rule and the failure it prevents.
- `core/config_schema.py`: the section classes and `Config`, whose pin
  refusal imports `ConfigInvalid` and `Remedy` locally (config imports it).
- `core/config_validate.py`: validation and the environment overrides.
- `core/taxonomy.py` (2,375 to 251) keeps the shipped templates and the
  loader, which the suite patches in place, and re-exports;
  `core/taxonomy_model.py` the constants and classes; `core/taxonomy_build.py`
  the build and its checks, with one reader for a folder node and one for an
  optional folder section.
- `core/cause.py` (2,526 to 1,394): prose only, every slug in its order.
- Re-exports were trimmed to the public names and the private ones something
  reaches; each new module has its own `foundation` or `domain` line in
  `tests/test_layering.py`'s placement table, beside its parent.
