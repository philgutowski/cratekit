**What landed.** Decided by Phillip on 2026-11-05, a twin's missing tracks

* **The hold lives on the pair.** `Pair.on_hold` in the pairs document, the
  remote ids a twin holds with the words that name each, written by
  `PairStore.mark`, validated on read, kept by a re-mark and by `record`.
  `crate sync twins` records each backfilled twin's misses as its hold
  (`sync/twins.pair_backfilled`); a landing's twin records an empty hold.
* **`crate sync pair` names a held track and queues none.**
  `pair_cli.held_for_choice`, read at the acquire decision after the queued
  before check, so a track an earlier sync queued is still named awaiting. A
  held track is an `on-hold` row on the gate, counted in `totals.on_hold`, in
  the plan's digest, and named per twin in the `sync-pair-on-hold` warning
  with the release command. A held track the library already holds is still
  added to the twin, as before.
* **`crate sync release ACCOUNT_ID... | --all`** (`sync/release_cli.py`, new)
  is the choice: it plans the twins named, refuses a name that is no twin's
  (`sync-release-not-a-twin`) and names with `--all`, or neither
  (`sync-release-unnamed`), shows each twin and each held track behind one
  explicit gate, `sync.release.approve`, and empties the hold of each twin
  still paired with the rekordbox playlist it planned
  (`PairStore.release_hold`). It writes the pairs document and nothing else;
  the queue stays `sync pair`'s, at its own gate. A twin paired again since
  the plan is named and left.
* **A twin that recorded no hold is read by time.** Sitting 63's 77 twins were
  paired before U221 and carry no hold, and neither does a twin made from a
  listing the provider served short. For those a track the library lacks is
  held unless the account dates its addition after the pair's `marked`; an
  undated track, or a mark that does not read, is held. So the 612 are held at
  the next sync with no migration, and a track Phillip adds to one of those
  playlists afterwards is queued as before.
