**The mechanism.** `cloud_state` reads Phillip's comments only as answers to
a `Brainstorm` or a `Question for Phillip`. "Stop" after a `Started`, "hold
off" on a fresh card, or "build it on fixtures" after `Needs the machine` all
leave the state as it was.

**Measured cost.** Driven at `cloud_state` for the three cases. It is the
rule METHOD states, and the skill now says the `attended` label or a close
is how he stops a card.

**Closes when:** a comment headed `Stop` or `Release` from him is read as a
state of its own, if that is wanted. Ledger only.
