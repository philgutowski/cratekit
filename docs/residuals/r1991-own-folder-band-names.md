---
title: A person's own folder under a governed root whose playlists bear band names and hold filed tracks reads not compared on every converge
found: at the review of #1022, R-1991, 2026-11-23.
issue: no
---

**The mechanism.** `convergence_check.unidentified` names a playlist under a
folder whose name declares no bucket when its own name is a declared
destination's playlist name (High, Med, Favorites, a genre sub) and it holds a
track the run files. A folder a person made by hand under the governed root,
`Wedding sets/High` say, meets that as a renamed genre folder does, since the
check cannot tell the two apart. The brief chose this: only a person's folder
whose playlists hold no filed track stays foreign.

**Measured cost.** Driven at both rounds with the folder made by a row write:
`converge` says `not-compared` for that tree on every run, naming the
playlist, and never `matching`; after round one's fix nothing is refused and
a rebuild still clears the flag. The same holds for an old renamed genre
folder once a rebuild or `adopt apply` made the genre's folder again beside
it, where neither act the wording names ends it. Whether the live tree holds
such a folder was not read.

**Closes when:** a person can mark a folder under the root as their own (or
the check learns the bucket another way), so its playlists leave the
comparison. Ledger only: a not compared line over a folder of the person's
own, with nothing refused, is not one of the board bar's four costs.
